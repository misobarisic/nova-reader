NOVA READER APPLICATION AND SHARED CRATES
Nova Reader and its reader-domain, reader-sources, reader-engine,
reader-downloads, reader-catalogue, reader-capture and UI crates are GPL-3.0-or-later.
Application/build/UI foundations derive from Nova revision
b69a1e5765ecc65ac3b00acdfe2f16e2f46a5dda. nova-storage, nova-ui-base,
nova-cache, nova-image and nova-sync are
consumed from nova-kit revision 096ec5e6c4cf2c30303a01eed42ca2debc165f56.
The generated Cargo catalog lists runtime crates for the actual target and
features; the complete project license and third-party notices follow.
