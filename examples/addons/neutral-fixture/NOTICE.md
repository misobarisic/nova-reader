# Neutral reader fixtures

Newly authored synthetic JavaScript and PNG images, dedicated to the public domain
under CC0-1.0 (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
No live-source material or adult content is included. Assets are geometric color
bands: portrait 600x900, wide 1800x600, tall 128x12000. The hundreds chapter has
320 stable ordered page descriptors reusing these three bundled images. Asset
URLs intentionally need no HTTP permissions and remain usable offline.
